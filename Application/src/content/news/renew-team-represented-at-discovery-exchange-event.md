We're excited to share that the [RENEWchallenge](https://www.linkedin.com/company/renewchallenge/) project was proudly represented by [Nicky Dawkins](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nicky-dawkins-0b895b212/), IRESI Lab Manager, and [Carrie Anne Barry](https://www.linkedin.com/in/carrie-anne-barry-9b192616/), COER Center Manager — both from [Maynooth University](https://www.linkedin.com/company/maynooth-university/) — at the recent Discovery Exchange event hosted by [Skillnet Innovation Exchange](https://www.linkedin.com/company/the-innovation-exchange/) and [Research Ireland](https://www.linkedin.com/company/research-ireland/).

As members of the RENEWchallenge team, Nicky and Carrie joined this initiative, which encourages industries to collaborate with research teams and explore new possibilities for innovation in the energy sector.

The RENEW project continues to drive energy innovation, and this event was a fantastic opportunity to foster research-industry partnerships and stakeholder engagement for a sustainable future. 🌍⚡
