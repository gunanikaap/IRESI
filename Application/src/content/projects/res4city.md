## Objective

The main goal of RES4CITY is to strengthen sustainable renewable energy and fuel technologies within urban areas. The project focuses on promoting sustainability, circularity, and innovative education designed together with stakeholders. By developing highly skilled human capital, RES4CITY supports the widespread adoption of renewable solutions across different educational systems.

## Impact

RES4CITY aims to create a scalable framework that accelerates the deployment of renewable energy technologies in cities. With increasing resource consumption in densely populated areas, the project provides tools and knowledge that support low-carbon urban development and contribute directly to Europe's green transition.

## Our Role

With a consortium of 18 partners across eight EU countries and Switzerland, RES4CITY works to close the skills gap in renewable energy and circularity.

The project combines research, education, and innovation to prepare professionals capable of driving sustainable change in modern cities.
