## Challenges and Impact

The challenges posed by traditional building practices are significant, including high energy consumption, resource depletion, and carbon emissions. However, the potential for positive change is equally significant. By addressing these challenges, we can create buildings that are not only environmentally friendly but also provide healthier and more comfortable spaces for occupants. The impact of sustainable building practices extends beyond energy efficiency; it encompasses improved indoor air quality, reduced operating costs, and a smaller ecological footprint.

## Research Focus

IRESI's research in the "Buildings" domain encompasses a wide range of areas:

- **Energy-Efficient Design:** We explore innovative architectural and engineering designs that prioritize energy efficiency, including passive design techniques and advanced insulation materials.
- **Smart Technologies:** Our research delves into the integration of smart technologies for building automation, optimizing energy consumption and enhancing occupant comfort.
- **Renewable Energy Integration:** We investigate the seamless integration of renewable energy sources such as solar panels and wind turbines into building structures.
- **Materials Innovation:** We study sustainable building materials and construction methods that reduce environmental impact and enhance building longevity.
