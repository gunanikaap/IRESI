## Objective

The energy system is a vital pillar of national infrastructure in all economies, and the research in this programme entails advanced modelling and optimisation of this system, investigating its future development and role in addressing the major energy challenges which face society.

The research programme is firmly within the energy field, addressing some of the major engineering research questions within the field of power and energy engineering, with the potential to significantly advance the state of the art and, through mathematical modelling, deliver key new insights into the challenges surrounding the decarbonisation of energy systems and pathways to the achievement of Net Zero Carbon Energy Systems.

## Impact

The programme also draws together a diverse range of disciplines to tackle the challenge of net zero from a technical, economic, and social point of view.

This interdisciplinary approach aims to provide a deeper understanding of decarbonisation pathways and contribute to the achievement of sustainable, net-zero energy systems.

## Our Role

In line with SFI's legal remit, the NexSys research programme comprises oriented basic research in the hub with complementary applied research in the targeted projects fully aligned with the Energy, Climate Action and Sustainability Refreshed Priority Theme, covering both the priority areas Decarbonising the Energy System and Sustainable Living.
