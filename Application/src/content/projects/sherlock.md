## Objective

The SHERLOCK project aims to boost innovation at European level by designing and implementing an original and advanced educational framework based on microcredentials for upskilling the workforce and lifelong learning.

It will integrate and combine a multidisciplinary green and digital skills portfolio to foster cooperation and knowledge exchange across stakeholders, supporting job creation and the EU's mass energy building retrofitting targets. SHERLOCK addresses the urgent need for a skilled workforce capable of managing the technical, financial and societal aspects of building energy retrofitting.

## Impact

The project contributes to the EU decarbonisation goals by 2050, addressing the building sector which accounts for 40% of energy consumption and 35% of carbon emissions.

By equipping professionals with green and digital skills, SHERLOCK helps bridge the gap between financial operators and energy project developers, ensuring a skilled workforce for the building renovation wave.

## Our Role

SHERLOCK will develop and deliver a MOOC-based MicroMaster programme and a VET-oriented training course designed with stakeholders and VET providers. The project will establish SHERLOCK Knowledge Centres as long-term hubs for collaboration between universities, businesses, and public institutions.

It will define learning objectives, design materials, and produce guidelines for educators on implementing innovative MicroMaster programmes that support lifelong learning and workforce upskilling.
