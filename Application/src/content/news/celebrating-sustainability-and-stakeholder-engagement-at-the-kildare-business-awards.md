The IRESI group at [School of Business Maynooth University](https://www.linkedin.com/company/school-of-business-maynooth-university/) was proud to represent the [RENEWchallenge](https://www.linkedin.com/company/renewchallenge/) Project at the prestigious Kildare Business Awards, where we were nominated in the Decarbonization Category.

The RENEW Project stands out for its dedication to stakeholder engagement, fostering collaboration to address critical challenges in energy transition and decarbonization. This recognition highlights the collective efforts of our team and partners in building a more sustainable future.

We were delighted to have our leadership team in attendance, including our guest of honor [Joseph Coughlan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/joseph-coughlan-016276b/), Head of School; [Fabiano Pallonetto](https://www.linkedin.com/in/fabianopallonetto/), our Director; [Amy Fahy](https://www.linkedin.com/in/amy-fahy-7236baba/), our Vice Director; along with [Nicky Dawkins](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nicky-dawkins-0b895b212/), our Lab Manager; [Carrie Anne Barry](https://www.linkedin.com/in/carrie-anne-barry-9b192616/), COER's Lab Manager; [Rebecca Doolin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rebecca-doolin-78aa175/), Vice President External Affairs; [Gordon Jamieson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gjamieson/), Senior Development Manager; [Peter McNamara](https://www.linkedin.com/in/peter-mcnamara-2035ab9/), Dean of the Faculty of Social Science; and Peter Conlon, Director of [MaynoothWorks](https://www.linkedin.com/company/maynoothworks/).

A heartfelt thank you to everyone who has supported this project, and congratulations to all the nominees and winners who are driving positive change in their fields. Here's to continued collaboration and innovation for a greener tomorrow! 💚🌍
