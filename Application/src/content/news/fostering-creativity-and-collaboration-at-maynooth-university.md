We're thrilled to share that our Vice Director, [Amy Fahy](https://www.linkedin.com/in/amy-fahy-7236baba/), Director [Fabiano Pallonetto](https://www.linkedin.com/in/fabianopallonetto/), and Lab Manager [Nicky Dawkins](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nicky-dawkins-0b895b212/) of the IRESI Group, recently hosted an engaging workshop at [School of Business Maynooth University](https://www.linkedin.com/company/school-of-business-maynooth-university/) as part of the [Creative Ireland](https://www.linkedin.com/company/creative-ireland/) Maynooth Climate Lab project. This session was focused on co-creation to drive innovative solutions for climate challenges.

Special thanks to [Christina Barry](https://www.linkedin.com/in/christina-barry-6ab734304/) and Philip O'Brien from the Creative Ireland project who shared their experiences of facilitating hands-on experiential learning workshops with local families, to explore concepts like sustainable energy, transport, circular economy, and biodiversity in a fun and interactive way.

The day ended on a festive and interactive note with a gingerbread-making competition! This unique activity brought participants together, fostering teamwork and showcasing how creativity can inspire collaboration in meaningful ways.

A heartfelt thank you to everyone who contributed to the success of this event. Your enthusiasm and ideas are driving forces in our mission for a more sustainable future.
