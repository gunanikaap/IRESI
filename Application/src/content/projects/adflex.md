## Objective

The ADFLEX project addresses key challenges in supporting Sustainable Energy Communities to provide energy flexibility and participate in local energy markets within mixed-use buildings. It integrates expertise from energy management, digital technology, and stakeholder engagement to develop an integrated, scalable solution validated through on-site pilot testing.

## Impact

ADFLEX contributes to national and EU energy goals by reducing emissions, enhancing renewable energy penetration, and improving grid flexibility at distribution and transmission levels. Its outcomes aim to accelerate the clean-energy transition and deliver high-impact, scalable results for communities across Europe.

## Our Role

ADFLEX brings together partners and stakeholders to co-develop and implement digital tools and smart energy frameworks that enhance coordination between communities, DSOs and TSOs. Through collaboration and validation within the Ringsend Sustainable Energy Community, the project ensures robust, real-world testing of its solutions.
