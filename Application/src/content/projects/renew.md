## Objective

The main goal of RENEW is to encourage carbon reduction, increase renewable energy adoption, and improve energy efficiency at the local level. By connecting technology, awareness, and participation, RENEW helps communities take ownership of their energy future.

## Impact

The project aims to create a replicable model for sustainable energy communities, demonstrating how digital innovation can drive both environmental and social benefits — paving the way for a cleaner, more resilient Europe.

## Our Role

Through interactive dashboards, learning modules, and real-time data visualization, RENEW supports citizens in managing their energy production and consumption effectively.

The platform is designed to be intuitive and inclusive — accessible to both younger audiences learning about sustainability and older users who may be less familiar with digital technologies. By combining simplicity with educational value, RENEW makes renewable energy understandable and engaging for everyone.
