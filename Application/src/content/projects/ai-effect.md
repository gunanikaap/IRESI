## Objective

AI-EFFECT aims to establish a European Testing and Experimentation Facility (TEF) for developing, testing, and validating AI applications in the energy sector.

The project connects existing facilities into a distributed digital platform to foster interoperability, flexibility, and scalability, becoming a central hub for collaboration across utilities, academia, and regulatory authorities.

## Impact

AI-EFFECT strengthens Europe's capacity for safe, interoperable, and certified AI technologies in the energy domain.

It promotes transparency, data integrity, and cross-sector innovation while aligning with the EU AI Act and Energy Data Spaces to ensure trustworthy and sustainable AI adoption.

## Our Role

The project develops and tests AI-driven use cases for district heating, grid congestion, distributed energy integration, and energy communities.

The consortium designs governance, certification, and business models ensuring AI-EFFECT's long-term sustainability beyond EU funding.
