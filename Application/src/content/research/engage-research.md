Engaged research describes a wide range of research approaches and methodologies that share a common interest in collaboration with societal partners, to address sustainability challenges. It is advanced with societal partners rather than for them (Campus Engage, 2022). At IRESI, our societal partners include service users, product users, policymakers, civil and civic society organizations, industry partners, members of the public, NGOs and other relevant stakeholders. Our societal partners are involved in the co-creation of knowledge, to address sustainable energy challenges.

## Challenges and Impact

The challenges we face in achieving a sustainable energy future are complex and interconnected. Engagement research allows us to confront these challenges head-on and measure our impact on the world. From reducing carbon emissions to enhancing energy efficiency, our research initiatives drive positive change.

## Research Focus

Our engagement research spans a wide range of areas, including renewable energy adoption, sustainable urban planning, community-driven projects, and more. We collaborate with diverse stakeholders to tackle pressing issues and develop innovative solutions. Our interdisciplinary approach ensures comprehensive, effective, and lasting outcomes.

## Collaboration Opportunities

IRESI thrives on collaboration. We welcome partnerships with industry, government agencies, nonprofits, and local communities. Together, we can leverage our combined expertise and resources to create sustainable solutions that transcend boundaries.

## Meeting the Challenge

IRESI is committed to developing relationships with our societal stakeholders through a variety of ways including dialogic workshops, community engagement initiatives, family outreach days, conference presentations, and contributions to government policy calls. In nurturing and developing these relationships, IRESI aims to provide all stakeholders with a voice to contribute to our research agenda. At IRESI, societal stakeholders have the opportunity to be involved throughout the research process, from the development of a research question to the dissemination of results and implementation of practical recommendations.

At IRESI, we recognize that research should be more than just an academic exercise. It should be a force for positive transformation. Explore our engagement research initiatives, learn about our ongoing projects, and discover how you can collaborate with us to make a tangible difference in the world of sustainable energy. Together, we can meet the challenges, forge solutions, and create a brighter, more sustainable future.

Several members of IRESI are Irish University Association (IUA) Campus Engage, Engaged Research Facilitators. The team is committed to providing Engaged Research Training and Development for all societal stakeholders involved in our projects.
