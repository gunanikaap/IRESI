## Challenges and Impact

The challenges facing electricity and power systems are multifaceted, from grid reliability and energy storage to grid integration and electrification of industries. Addressing these challenges is paramount to reducing carbon emissions, increasing energy efficiency, and ensuring a resilient energy infrastructure. Our research in this domain has a far-reaching impact, not only in reducing environmental harm but also in enhancing energy accessibility and affordability for all.

## Research Focus

Within our Electricity and Power Systems division, we delve into critical areas such as:

- **Grid Management:** Developing advanced techniques for grid optimization, stability, and resilience.
- **Energy Storage Solutions:** Exploring innovative energy storage technologies to balance supply and demand.
- **Grid Integration:** Integrating renewable energy sources seamlessly into existing grids.
- **Electrification:** Promoting electrification of transportation and industrial processes to reduce reliance on fossil fuels.

## Collaboration Opportunities

Collaboration is at the heart of our research approach. We actively seek partnerships with academic institutions, industry leaders, and governmental bodies to foster innovation and drive positive change. Together, we can address the complex challenges facing electricity and power systems and create scalable solutions for a sustainable energy future.
